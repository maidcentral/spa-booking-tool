# Product Mission (Lite)

MaidCentral Booking Tool is a white-label embeddable booking widget that helps MaidCentral partners migrate from BookingKoala without losing functionality by providing a modern, customizable booking solution that seamlessly integrates into their websites.

MaidCentral Booking Tool serves cleaning service businesses who need a professional online booking solution that maintains their brand identity. Unlike BookingKoala and generic booking widgets, we provide complete customization with partner-specific theming, dynamic configuration, and scalable iframe embedding that supports hundreds of partner instances simultaneously.
